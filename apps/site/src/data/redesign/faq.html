Pay at the studio or online in the app. Online payments are processed by Razorpay, and you get an invoice for every completed job.

What is AutoDeck Club?

Club plans last 30 days and bundle a set number of washes and pickups for one price. They belong to your account, so you can use them for any car in your garage. See Club in the app
