# Original gifted-zero cancellation, local and dormant

No production route imports this writer. A separate exact private review binds
the recipient's zero-price service, gift redemption, buyer's original funding
and any refund activity. The buyer payment is evidence for gift restoration,
not a recipient receipt. No gift purchase/refund is performed.

A qualifying cancellation restores only the original paid gift identity and
same claimed recipient, retaining the original expiry. An expired restored gift
therefore stays expired. Original buyer funding and sibling obligations stay
unchanged. A refunded or void gift is never revived. Pending/unknown refund or
external-return activity, missing/duplicate/wrong buyer funding, transport,
additional work or a final existing policy fee stays held.

An exact retry returns the original cancellation result and cannot restore a
credit twice or undo a later legitimate redemption. No new gift, wash count,
cash receipt, buyer refund, expiry or different recipient is created.

This narrow slice covers zero-recipient-price, unissued gifted work without
additional work or physical transport. General benefits, extra charges, final
fees and connected visible controls/reporting remain unfinished. Nothing here
changes UI or decides issued-invoice/tax treatment. The complete current routed
before/after review and owner approval remain required before live use.
